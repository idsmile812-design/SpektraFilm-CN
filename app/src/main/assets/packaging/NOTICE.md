The app bundles packaging illustrations for all twenty film stocks the workbench can
select, drawn by SpektraFilm CN contributors. They are not product photographs, not scans,
and not exact reproductions of any current or historical package. No old product photograph,
screenshot, or reference image is used as artwork. Each 1200 × 600 JPEG keeps only the real
brand, series, and model plus an identifying colour family; the ISO/EI, 35 mm / 135,
DAYLIGHT/TUNGSTEN, and format copy on the ivory technical panel comes from this repository's
stock catalogue and does not imitate an official layout.

Vision3 2383 / 2393 是印刷胶片，Endura 与 Crystal Archive 是相纸，它们都不是胶卷，

Motion-picture negatives are labelled as CINE and do not carry a mechanical "36 EXP"
line; only 35 mm still films do. Vision3 2383 / 2393 are print films and Endura /
Crystal Archive are print papers, not camera films, so no film-can packaging is invented
for them; they keep their clearly labelled information cards. Stocks such as Pro 400H that
previously had no artwork and fell back to a card now have their own original illustration.
All twenty images are reproducible from the generator.
